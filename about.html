About AIM Enterprises

AIM Enterprises is a Canon Authorised Business Partner & Service Provider based in Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair). We sell, install and service Canon office equipment for businesses, schools, banks and government departments across the Andaman & Nicobar Islands.

Being on the islands means our customers cannot wait for a service engineer to fly in. We keep stock, spares and trained people here, so a machine bought from us is looked after locally for its whole working life.

Alongside Canon, we supply IT peripherals such as SSDs, UPS units, monitors and keyboards, and provide Lenovo and Wipro service support.
