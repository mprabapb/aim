One Camera. Many Stories.

Capture once is all you need with EOS R6 V's 7K 30p RAW Open Gate, which gives you the freedom to reframe for widescreen, vertical social and tighter crops — all from the same footage. An internal cooling fan extends recording and streaming time, while 7K 60p RAW Light, uncropped 4K 120p and Canon Log 2/3 give creators tools to shape their vision with depth and creative control.

The EOS R6 V's In-Body Image Stabilizer reduces camera shake for steadier handheld video and sharper stills — with up to 40fps burst shooting at 32.5MP.

Key Features

7K 30p RAW Open Gate with Internal Cooling Fan & In-Body Image Stabilizer

7K 60p RAW (Light), 4K 120p and Canon Log 2 & 3 with Dual Pixel CMOS AF II

Up to 40 fps Continuous Shooting with 32.5MP Full-Frame CMOS Sensor

Many Possibilities

Many Perspectives

Capture once. Create more. With EOS R6 V's 7K 30p RAW open gate recording, the full-frame sensor gives an output at 3:2 and you have the freedom to craft cinematic 16:9 films, vertical 9:16 social edits, square 1:1 cuts, tighter crops, zooms and pans from the same high-resolution footage. Achieve multiple perspectives with the same footage for many stories.

The only limit to how the story gets told is your imagination.

Many Outputs

Whatever the scene demands, the EOS R6 V is ready. With a versatile range of recording formats built for quality, clarity and creative control, every shoot gives you more to work with and more ways to tell your story.

7K 60p RAW

Capture high-resolution 7K 60p RAW (Light) internally in full-frame 17:9, preserving rich detail and depth for serious post-production.

It balances image quality with more efficient file handling, while support for Canon RAW development and NLE plug-ins give creators greater flexibility from shoot to final edit.

4K 60p / 30p Oversampled

Create detailed 4K footage with oversampling at 60p Fine and up to 7K oversampling in 4K 30p Fine. With more data than the final resolution requires, it produces cleaner, more detailed images with smoother gradations and more accurate colours. 

*Image quality differs between 4K 60p Fine and 4K 30p Fine.

4K 120p

Capture smooth slow-mo sequences in stunning 4K — with full autofocus, exposure tracking, and up to 5x slower playback.

The full horizontal readout and advanced processing deliver razor-sharp detail at 120fps, with no crop.

2K 180p / FHD 180p

Make moments go up to 7.5x slower with 2K or Full HD 180p recording for added impact and detail — with audio captured, your footage is ready to use straight out of the camera. 

*Recording is cropped.

Cinematic Colour Control

Shape stories with Canon Log 2 & 3, giving you film-like latitude with up to 15+ stops of dynamic range* and seamless integration with the Cinema EOS series. For faster productions, the EOS R6 V also supports Canon 709, HDR PQ and HLG profiles, helping creators achieve polished results straight out of camera.

With Custom Picture, Look Files (LUT) and in-camera Colour Filters, creators can preview, refine and maintain their intended look from shoot to edit. Whether building a graded cinematic workflow or creating distinctive tones without heavy post-production, EOS R6 V gives creators flexible colour control in one camera.

*Canon Log 2 (at 4K 30p FINE, ISO800)

Many Guides

Know your exposure, nail your focus and read your light — all in real time. The EOS R6 V puts the right visual information in front of you as you create, so you stay in control of your light, exposure and focus for every story.

Waveform

Monitor your brightness levels in real time with line display and RGB parade, so every lighting decision on set is precise, intentional and exactly how you want it.

False Colour

See your exposure mapped across six luminance-based colours, allowing you to match the exposure of your subject effortlessly at a glance in real time.

Zebra Display

Stay ahead of blown highlights with instant zebra stripe overlays on overexposed areas, keeping your exposure in check and your footage well exposed.

Focus Guide

Take full command of your focus with a live visual guide that shows direction of movement and distance to subject, so every manual pull lands exactly where it should.

Proxy & Sub Recording

Record 7K RAW or 4K DCI to CFexpress while simultaneously capturing lightweight 2K DCI proxy or 4K sub files to the SD card. Shared timecode and file names keep both recordings in perfect sync for a smooth post-production workflow.

Apply Look Files (LUT) to your proxies so editors can see your intended look from the start, while the original RAW file stays fully flexible for final grading.

LPCM / 24-bit / 4-Ch Audio

Record studio-quality 24-bit audio with flexible 4-channel input support. The multi-channel audio system allows you to combine external microphones, the built-in mic and multiple sources at once, making it ideal for interviews with clear dialogue, rich ambient recordings, live performances with separate instrument and vocal tracks, or director's commentary captured on the go.

Inside Story

Powering the EOS R6 V is a 32.5MP full-frame CMOS sensor working in tandem with Canon's DIGIC X processor — engineered to reproduce every detail, tone and colour with remarkable precision and depth. Whether you're shooting fast action or expansive scenes, the resolution and cropping flexibility are there to help you frame and capture the story the way you intended.

14-bit RAW

Retain the full breadth of dynamic range, colour depth and image data for complete creative control in post.

14-bit C-RAW

Everything RAW delivers, in files approximately 40% smaller for faster workflows and lesser storage.

10-bit HEIF

Richer colours and smoother gradients than JPEG, producing lifelike images straight from the camera with no extra steps.

8-bit JPEG

Instantly shareable files processed in-camera, looking great straight out of camera for your usage.

Every Frame is a Story

Capture at up to 40 frames per second with electronic shutter — silent, blackout-free and built to keep up with whatever that unfolds in front of you. The Pre-continuous Shooting saves up to 20 frames before you fully press the shutter, even the moments you didn't anticipate. Whether it is a bird taking flight, a fleeting expression or a split-second in sports, no story gets untold.

Shoot in RAW, C-RAW, HEIF or JPEG — whatever fits your workflow.

Keeping the Story in Focus

The Dual Pixel CMOS AF II delivers fast, accurate subject detection and tracking across stills and video. Powered by Canon's Deep Learning AF, the EOS R6 V recognises and follows people, animals and vehicles, holding focus on faces, eyes, heads and bodies even as subjects move unpredictably. Every expression, every decisive moment, captured with confidence.

Register People Priority lets you store up to 100 faces, so the camera automatically recognises and prioritises the right subjects — freeing you to focus on telling the story.

Keep focus locked when presenting products close to the camera. AF for close-up demo helps the EOS R6 V shift focus smoothly between the creator and nearby objects, making reviews, tutorials and product shots easier to film solo.

In-Body
Image Stabilizer

The EOS R6 V's built-in 5-axis In-Body Image Stabilizer reduces camera shake across every lens — delivering stable, smooth footage. Pair it with a compatible RF lens and Coordinated Control IS combines In-Body Image Stabilizer (IS) and Optical Image Stabilizer (IS) for even greater shake reduction, extending correction up to 7.5 stops1 for handheld telephoto and long-exposure shooting that once required a tripod.

For video, Coordinated Control IS with Movie Digital IS brings all three systems — In-Body IS, Optical IS and Movie Digital — together, minimising camera shake and movement across the peripheral area and keeping footage smooth and natural even while walking or shooting handheld. From handheld 7K RAW to fast-moving run-and-gun shoots, motion remains natural and fluid throughout. Every story comes out cinematic, stable and ready to use.

Keeping the Camera Cool

In-Built Cooling Fan

Keep creating for longer with the EOS R6 V's internal cooling fan, designed to manage heat during extended movie recording and streaming. Adjustable fan settings allow you greater control over cooling performance, helping to maintain a smoother, more reliable shooting experience.

Zooming into Stories

Smooth, intentional zoom movements can completely transform the way a story feels. With built-in zoom lever support for compatible Power Zoom lenses and RF lenses that can pair with Power Zoom Adapter (PZ-E2 / PZ-E2B), the EOS R6 V puts precise control of every push-in, pull-out and reframe right at your fingertips.

Fine-tune your zoom speed across 15 levels in both slow and fast modes to match the emotion of the scene — from slow, deliberate transitions that draw the viewer in, to faster, more dynamic reframes that shift the energy in an instant. It's one more way the EOS R6 V expands your creative language and gives you greater freedom to tell your story exactly as you feel it.

Intuitive Creator Operability

The EOS R6 V keeps essential controls exactly where you need them. A video and stills switch, 7 buttons with expanded customisation options, dedicated dials and a front recording button let you respond quickly whether shooting solo, handheld or on a rig. The Vari-angle LCD adds further flexibility, adapting to your shooting position wherever the scene takes you.

From adjusting exposure to starting a take from the front of the camera or moving into slow or fast motion, every control is designed for faster, more instinctive movie production.

Dual Card Slots

The EOS R6 V offers flexible dual card recording with one CFexpress Type B slot and one SD UHS-II slot, supporting high-performance recording while keeping workflows efficient.

Record RAW to CFexpress and sub or proxy movies to the SD card, record stills and movies separately, continue with relay recording, or create backup recordings across both cards for greater production security.

Strong & Durable

A rigid magnesium-alloy chassis and top cover give the EOS R6 V dependable strength without adding bulk — keeping it compact and ready for handheld, gimbal and rig-based shooting across studio, outdoor and on-location productions.

Dust- & Drip-Resistant Sealing

Built for real-world workflows, the EOS R6 V features dust- and drip-resistant sealing across key areas including the battery compartment, card slot and rotating controls. Even the cooling fan carries the same protection, giving you greater confidence when conditions change.

Native Vertical Mounting

A dedicated vertical tripod socket lets you mount the EOS R6 V directly in portrait orientation — no additional rigging needed. Pair it with a gimbal for a cleaner vertical setup, while the display and user interface on the camera rotates automatically for easier operation.

Connectivity

image.canon Cloud

The EOS R6 V connects seamlessly to image.canon, Canon's free cloud platform built for backup and collaboration. Original files upload automatically and are sorted intelligently by subject, face, and sharpness — saving hours of post-shoot organisation.

Transfer instantly to Google Photos, Adobe Lightroom, or Frame.io for remote editing and team workflows. With automatic uploads from camera or smartphone and unlimited 30-day storage, your images stay safe and accessible wherever you work.

*Information is subject to change. Refer to https://image.canon/ for the latest info.

Wireless Connectivity

Built-in Wi-Fi (5GHz/2.4GHz) and Bluetooth Low Energy 5.1 keep the EOS R6 V constantly connected to your smartphone via Canon Camera Connect for instant sharing and remote control.

Switch effortlessly from low-power pairing to high-speed Wi-Fi with improved 5GHz handover support, or rely on stable, wired USB-C when maximum speed matters. Browse, transfer, or upload directly to social media without removing the card—built for creators who move fast.

*Compatible with iOS and Android devices. WPA3-Personal supported for secure wireless communication.

Pro Network Integration

The EOS R6 V supports FTP / FTPS transfer for direct server delivery and IEEE 802.11a/ac (5GHz) high-speed communication for studio and field workflows. Pair it with Canon's Content Transfer Professional app to move large files via 5G smartphones — complete with metadata tags and voice memos for streamlined newsroom or event coverage.

For live production, UVC / UAC streaming enables 4K output through a single USB-C cable, while the multi-function shoe delivers clean audio and power transmission.

*Some features may require accessories or subscriptions; availability varies by region.
