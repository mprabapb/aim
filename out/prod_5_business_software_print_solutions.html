Cloud Connector

Enhance convenience, drive productivity, and reduce IT burden to lower organisational costs, especially for smaller businesses.

Features

Seamless Cloud Connectivity

Connect your Canon devices to email and seven leading cloud repositories—Google Drive, OneDrive, OneDrive for Business, SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, Dropbox, and Box—for instant access to your documents anytime, anywhere.

Boost Productivity

Easily print from and scan to the cloud, making document access quicker than before. By eliminating extra steps and reducing delays, work gets done faster and more efficiently.

Fast, Secure Printing Made Easy

Link your email with a secure PIN for instant, worry-free printing.
