Features

Performance That Delivers

The imageFORCE 1440 is built to keep pace with busy offices, producing up to 40 pages per minute with consistent, high‑quality output. Automatic duplex printing and versatile paper handling streamline workflows, while fast warm‑up times ensure the device is ready for use.

Smooth User Experience

The imageFORCE 1440 is designed to make everyday tasks effortless. A responsive 5‑inch colour touch panel and intuitive shortcuts simplify printing, copying, and scanning, ensuring that anyone can operate the device with ease. Clear navigation and smart design deliver a seamless experience that keeps workflows moving without interruption.

Trusted Security

Safeguard sensitive information with advanced protection features. User authentication prevents unauthorised access, while system verification ensures firmware integrity, giving businesses confidence in secure document handling.
